## One marketplace for Zambia

Convergeo brings **products, services and events** into a single, fast, mobile-first app.
Compare vendors selling the same product, request quotes for services, or buy tickets to
events — all priced in Kwacha.

## Why people use Convergeo

- **Escrow on online payments** — mobile money and card payments stay held by Convergeo until you confirm delivery.
- **Cash on delivery** — still available on eligible orders when you prefer to pay on arrival.
- **Built for slow networks** — light pages that load fast on 3G.
- **Order updates** — follow every step in your account and by email.
