## Cash on delivery (COD)

You pay cash when your order is delivered. Nothing is charged online, and you only pay
once you have your order in your hands.

## Eligible orders

Cash on delivery is available for eligible orders. If an order is not eligible, checkout
tells you before you place it.

## Online payment

Mobile money and card payments are **coming soon**. Until then, cash on delivery is the way
to pay.
