## Cash on delivery (COD)

You pay cash when your order is delivered. Nothing is charged online for a COD order — you pay once you have your order in your hands.

## Eligible orders

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to **K500**. If your total is higher, checkout offers MTN or Airtel Mobile Money or card with escrow instead. If COD is not available for your cart, checkout tells you before you place it.

## Larger orders

For orders above the COD cap, pay with **MTN or Airtel Mobile Money** or **card** at checkout. Your payment is held by Convergeo until you confirm delivery.
