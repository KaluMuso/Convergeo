## Cash on delivery (COD)

You pay cash when your order is delivered. Nothing is charged online for a COD order — you
only pay once you have your order in your hands.

## Eligible orders

Cash on delivery is available for eligible orders up to the platform limit. If an order is
not eligible, checkout tells you before you place it and you can pay online instead.

## Larger orders

When you pay with mobile money or card, your payment is held in escrow until you confirm
delivery — see **How escrow protects your money on Vergeo5**.
