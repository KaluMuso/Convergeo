## Where to see your order

Open **your account** to see every order and its current status — from _Confirmed_ to
_Delivered_. You will also get updates **by email** as the status changes.

## Common statuses

- **Confirmed** — the vendor is preparing your order.
- **Shipped / Ready for pickup** — on the way or ready to collect.
- **Delivered** — received; pay the cash amount to the courier.

Not seeing an update you expected? Message us on WhatsApp with your order number.
