## Ways to pay

- **MTN or Airtel Mobile Money** — approve a push prompt on your phone. Your payment is held in escrow until you confirm delivery.
- **Debit or credit card** — pay on Lenco's secure card page. We never see your card number.
- **Cash on delivery** — pay the rider in cash when your order arrives. Available for eligible orders up to **K500**.

## Escrow on MoMo and card

When you pay with MoMo or card, Convergeo holds the money until you confirm delivery. The seller is paid only after that.

## Prices are always in Kwacha

Every listing shows the price in ZMW, including any delivery fee before you pay.
