## Ways to pay

- **Cash on delivery** — available now. Pay when your order arrives.
- **Mobile money and card** — coming soon. We will announce them once they are switched on.

During the pilot you do not pay anything online.

## Prices are always in Kwacha

Every listing shows the price in ZMW, including any delivery fee before you pay.
