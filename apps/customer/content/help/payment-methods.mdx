## Ways to pay

- **Mobile money** — MTN and Airtel push to your phone at checkout. Funds go into escrow until you confirm delivery.
- **Card** — pay through our secure hosted checkout when card is offered.
- **Cash on delivery** — on eligible orders, pay cash when your order arrives. Checkout tells you before you place the order.

## Prices are always in Kwacha

Every listing shows the price in ZMW, including any delivery fee before you pay.
