**D28 — B2B wholesale gating (strategy-alignment audit, 2026-07-14; amended 2026-08-05):** wholesale supply pricing (tier prices + MOQ) and the wholesale discovery feed are **hidden until a buyer is a verified business**, never applied on the listing alone. A buyer-side `business_buyers` identity (PACRA reg + optional TPIN) carries a `pending→verified/rejected/suspended` lifecycle (status server-controlled; admin verifies). A single shared resolver (`is_verified_business` / `app/services/business/access.py`) is the eligibility gate, enforced identically at discovery, cart, and checkout. **Dual-mode listings** (retail price _and_ wholesale tiers): a guest or non-verified consumer sees **retail pricing only**; a verified business buyer sees tier pricing and MOQ inline. **Wholesale-only listings** (no retail price): **MUST return `404` (non-disclosing) to retail consumers** on every touch — discovery, direct fetch, and cart entry — not retail pricing and not `403`. **Why `404`, not retail or refusal:** (1) **B2B data leakage** — showing retail pricing on a wholesale-only line, or returning `403`, confirms the id names a real listing and lets id enumeration map the B2B catalogue, its vendors, and by inference its pricing structure; (2) **cart logic hole** — any consumer path that admits a wholesale-only listing allows `POST /cart/items` at retail with no MOQ, because cart and checkout paths persist `unit_price_ngwee` and do not always re-derive eligibility on read. Omission + not-found closes both leaks. **`403` is reserved** for the explicit B2B feed (`/catalog/listings?wholesale=true`), where the caller asserted wholesale intent (see D36). **Follow-up (2026-07-15):** "hidden" now spans **every** consumer discovery surface, not just the dedicated supplies feed — wholesale-only listings are omitted from the catalog PLP, product detail, price comparison, vendor storefront profile, and FTS search/suggest for guests & non-verified consumers (verified businesses still see them inline via `get_business_access`), and dropped unconditionally from "Ask Vergeo" retrieval (query-keyed cache). The onboarding vendor **archetype** is persisted on the vendor row (not localStorage-only) and now drives a tailored vendor-home quick-start card. This is the thin, present-day slice; the **full B2B stack stays OUT of v1** per the scope fence below (credit/Net terms, buyer organisations & roles, account managers, contract pricing, multi-warehouse + lot/batch, wallet/financing) — all Phase 2.

## K. B2B visibility & social-commerce scope (2026-08-01; D28 amended 2026-08-05)

**D36 — Wholesale visibility: omission, not refusal (amends D28, 2026-08-01; D28 text amended 2026-08-05).** D28 now distinguishes **dual-mode** from **wholesale-only** listings (see D28). This decision makes the wholesale-only rule operational and testable, because the codebase had been carrying both behaviours under one sentence. **A dual-mode listing** (retail price _and_ wholesale tiers) is unchanged: a guest or non-verified consumer sees the **retail price inline**, a verified business sees tier pricing and MOQ, and eligibility is resolved only through `app/services/business/access.py`. **A wholesale-only listing** (no retail price) is **omitted entirely** from every consumer surface — catalog PLP and facet counts, product detail, price comparison, vendor storefront, FTS search and suggest, "Ask Vergeo" retrieval, and **cart add/update/merge** — and a direct fetch by id returns **`404`, not `403`**. **The reason is disclosure, not politeness:** a `403` or any visible consumer price confirms that the id names a real listing, so anyone able to enumerate ids can map the entire B2B catalogue — its size, its vendors, and by inference its pricing structure — without ever qualifying as a business buyer; admitting the listing to the cart path re-opens the retail-at-no-MOQ hole D28 names. A `404` is indistinguishable from a listing that never existed and leaks nothing. **`403` is reserved for the explicit B2B feed** (`/catalog/listings?wholesale=true`), where the caller has asserted business intent and refusing is an answer to a question actually asked, not an unsolicited confirmation. Wholesale controls do not ship until this behaviour is proven by a guest × consumer × verified-business × wholesale-only/dual-mode test matrix. **Scope-fence amendment:** multi-warehouse/lots and wholesale RFQ move from D28's Phase-2 OUT list **into R02 scope**; credit/Net terms, buyer organisations & roles, account managers, and wallet/financing remain OUT.

**D37 — Social commerce, not WeChat (2026-08-01; formalized 2026-08-05).** Vergeo5 builds a **social-commerce layer anchored to inventory**, not a WeChat-style social network or general messaging platform. **APPROVED for v1:** listing-anchored **customer-to-business (C2B) enquiries** with **vendor replies**; **saves and follows**; **sharing product, event, and clip links externally** (deep links / OS share sheet — not an in-app public feed); gift purchase/redemption later. **EXPLICITLY BANNED FOR v1:** general **customer-to-customer (C2C) DMs**; open **WeChat-style public user profiles**; **public user feeds**; **arbitrary user-to-user image exchange**; groups and broadcast channels. **Reason:** mitigates moderation, privacy, and fraud risks — grooming, CSAM-reporting, Zambia DPA obligations over private message content, and realtime infrastructure — while respecting the solo-founder operational constraint and the **$50/mo** ceiling. A marketplace's moderation load scales with **orders**; an open social network's scales with **users**. Listing-anchored C2B enquiries capture most of the commercial value — pre-purchase questions are what convert — at a fraction of that surface, because every thread has a business on one end and an order-shaped context. **Structural guarantee:** the schema must make a customer↔customer thread _unrepresentable_, not merely unreachable by the UI; a thread is rooted at a listing/event/service and one party is always the vendor. **WAHA remains excluded from every customer-facing channel** (D15 and D35 unchanged): enquiry and reply notifications ride the **official WhatsApp Cloud API through the existing outbox**, subject to its consent and template controls. An LLM may draft or suggest, but never auto-sends on a vendor's behalf and never moderates.
