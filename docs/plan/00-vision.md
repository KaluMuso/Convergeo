# Convergeo — vision (founder, 13 Nov 2026)

> "Convergeo is the Hub of logos. These logos are different businesses and companies all fitting seamlessly and finding a home on Convergeo — whether products, services, events, inventory. Every business must be able to bring its story to buy and sell, to discover and grow. Let Africa shine through Convergeo."
> — Kaluba Prosper Musonda (King), founder

Reference image: a Lusaka shopping street — a long row of shopfronts, each with its own sign, side by side under one roof (`vision-storefront-street.jpg`).

## What this means for every design and build decision

1. **Every business keeps its own identity.** Storefronts show the business's logo, colours, story and photos first; Convergeo is the street, not the shop sign.
2. **Every kind of business fits.** Retail, wholesale/inventory, services, events and organisers each get a home that feels made for them — not a product page bent to fit.
3. **Discovery is the street walk.** Customers browse shops side by side, find who is near them, and move between products, services and events without friction.
4. **Growth is visible to the business.** Each business sees its own sales, customers, stock and performance, and the tools to grow (promotion, bulk pricing, Clips).
5. **Simple for everyone.** Plain words, icons and one main action per screen; the platform does the heavy lifting in the background.
6. **Made in Zambia, built for Africa.** Mobile-money first, cash where needed, works on slow networks, and ready to expand across the region.
